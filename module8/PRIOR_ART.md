# D5 literature decision — checked 2026-10-27

Florentino, Romão and Silva, [EPJC 81 (2021) 1148](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09956-2),
[arXiv:2106.08332](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.08332), already propose the
heavy charged scalar → light charged scalar + Z cascade followed by tb (Sec. 7.1).
Sec. 7.6/Fig. 14 uses referenced tbH production cross sections; Sec. 7.7 obtains
cascade BRs above 70% with the light scalar predominantly decaying to tb.
Sec. 8/conclusions explicitly leaves neutrino/flavor constraints for future work.
Neither this cascade, its BR hierarchy, the off-diagonal Z coupling nor tbH
production is a new signal here. Their historical exclusions do not establish
acceptance of our benchmarks under current constraints.

[Herrero-García et al., arXiv:1701.05345v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.05345v2)
already performs a general-Zee scan involving neutrino masses and lepton-flavor
observables. General complex leptonic Yukawas and flavor correlations require
comparison with that work; merely allowing those matrices is insufficient novelty.

[Banerjee et al., arXiv:2501.09796v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.09796v1)
studies charged-scalar DY production, discusses mixing between different isospin
components, and specifically lists the Zee model. Its displayed mixed-state
examples concern diagonal physical-state pairs; this tight check did not identify
an explicit unequal H1±H2∓ hadronic rate there. That observation is not a proof
that such a calculation has never appeared.

Targeted searches included `"Zee model" "charged scalar" "Drell-Yan"`,
`"mixed pair" "Zee"`, `"H_1^+ H_2^-" "production"`,
`"H1+H2-" production`, `"charged scalar" "unequal" "production"`,
and off-diagonal charged-Higgs pair-production variants. Inspected primary
sources above establish related prior art, but no exhaustive bibliographic or
citation-chain proof of absence. **D5_NOVELTY_NOT_ESTABLISHED** remains.
The mixing coefficient itself is established prior art even if a particular
unequal-pair benchmark rate were absent from those papers.

At fixed masses and mixing, general lepton Yukawas do not change our Z-only
hard amplitude or Type-II gb amplitude. They change allowed parameter populations
and decay branching fractions. D4's twelve inverse families produce only minute
BR shifts and do not establish measurable flavor discrimination. A useful first
paper requires new, quantitatively constrained flavor–collider correlations,
real neutrino/LFV constraints and actual rates. D5 supplies reference LO rates;
it does not close LFV, physical B→Xsγ, vacuum, detector or global viability gates.
