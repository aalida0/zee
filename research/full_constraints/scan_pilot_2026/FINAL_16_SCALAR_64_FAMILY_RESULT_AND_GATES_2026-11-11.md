### Published B theory definition and independent backend

From Misiak et al, arXiv:2608.29863v1, p.4 Eq.(4):
```
10^4 × BR(B->Xs gamma, E_gamma > 1.6 GeV)
  = (3.54 ± 0.14) − 8.51 Delta C7(160 GeV) − 2.16 Delta C8(160 GeV)
```
For the real Type-II Zee quark sector:
`DeltaCi = cos(gamma)^2 * DeltaCi_II(mH1) + sin(gamma)^2 * DeltaCi_II(mH2)`
at *the retained leading electroweak / leading BSM Wilson order*.
The authors **explicitly neglect quadratic DeltaC7,8** in this formula.
Our matching has separate original BMU/NDR and native consistency checks.
An effective Gaussian pull used here assumes independent quoted SM
(±0.14)e-4 and experiment (±0.19)e-4 errors with zero extra
NP uncertainty. **Not a full Zee likelihood, uncertainty or exclusion.**
The published ordinary *one-state* Type-II 2HDM mH+ > 670 GeV bound
does **not** directly constrain the two mixed Zee charged masses.

Independent flavio 2.7.1 (`particle==0.26.2`) WET C7/C8 rate evaluations
serve as a **method-difference test**, not an updated matching NNLO rate;
its own SM baseline 3.3096922701754877e-4 differs from the 2026 SM
baseline 3.54e-4, so ONLY the NP shift is transferred and explicitly
labeled heuristic. Its used NLO dipole-only variant **does not include
ordinary C4_NLO**, C1–6 mixing uncertainties, or a NP perturbative covariance.
