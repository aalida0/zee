## Minimum inputs for a decision

1. Provide accessible files and exact dataset/production metadata for 522024/522028 and relevant additional backgrounds; establish actual energy, event counts, cross section, k-factor convention, filter efficiency, generator/shower/PDF and nominal/systematic weights with signed sumw and sumw². Establish HF definitions, ttZ inclusive/HF and tWZ overlap, statistical precision in the three-b tails, plus a nonprompt estimate. A file listing alone is insufficient.
2. Restore the pinned native signal generator/UFO and original certified family cards; validate H2→H1Z→tbZ decays and generate at most 2,000 events each for 15591 and 2264 at the **same** energy as the available background, retaining hashes and decay normalization. Use a common source-bound Delphes card and selections, with weighted cutflows, mass closure, efficiencies and uncertainties.
3. Only after sufficient selected background and signal statistics and an explicit nuisance model exist, construct a reproducible likelihood with MC statistical uncertainty and correlated background systematics. Until then there is no expected 95% CLs, exclusion or HL-LHC reach. A 13.6 TeV pilot cannot use 14 TeV signal rates.

## Paper methodology

Use scalar tree BFB/unitarity/perturbativity, scoped one-loop oblique and neutrino closure, and a **positive sufficient tree-vacuum certificate** as stated-domain selections. Missing vacuum certificate is UNKNOWN, not a failure. Coherent two-state B→Xsγ and partial LFV results are diagnostics with reference scales, not physical vetoes; μ→e conversion and incomplete Higgs search coverage remain UNKNOWN. `global_acceptance=null`. No single-state Type-II mass bound is imposed separately on the mixed states. A future validated physical failing observable can exclude a point, but diagnostic subsets cannot establish global acceptance. Do not splice the D12 F13 flavor texture onto a different D14 production spectrum.

The [2017 Zee neutrino/LFV study](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP04(2017)130), [2021 charged-scalar cascade study](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-021-09956-2), and [ATLAS ttll analysis](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14695-9) supply comparison and channel motivation; none validates this proposed selection or supplies a transferable limit.
