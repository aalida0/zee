## Additional 2024–2026 search on continuation

A second provider, alphaXiv, was queried for `Zee model`, `charge breaking`, `vacuum stability`, `charged scalar` on 8 November 2026, restricted to 1 January 2024–8 November 2026, ranked by recency. It returned nine ranked candidates, not a complete result count; eight obvious nonmatches included a 331 model, a real-singlet SM extension and general vacuum decay. The one direct Zee-field-content candidate was read in full: Jin, Nomura and Okada, [arXiv:2605.23202v1](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.23202), Sec. II, Eqs. (2)–(3), (7)–(8), and reference [3]. Their model contains two doublets and a charged singlet, with non-invertible-symmetry restrictions on the leptonic Yukawa textures and a leptophilic choice for the non-SM doublet. Their displayed Eq. (3) includes a doublet/singlet cubic but omits explicit singlet mass and self/portal terms; it must not be treated as the complete frozen SARAH potential. They state they do not analyze the scalar potential in detail, and focus on neutrino and lepton-flavor predictions. This is a field-content overlap, not a demonstrated global-vacuum certificate or a source-compatible numerical limit. Their reference [3] is Herrero-García et al. 2017, supplying one additional backward citation edge. No transferable scalar-vacuum limiting case was presented or claimed here. The other eight ranked candidates were excluded by titles/abstracts only, not certified as irrelevant by full-text review. Two atlas files were also attempted via alphaXiv's GitHub reader; both returned repository inaccessible. This targeted search does not close the incomplete INSPIRE forward citation graph or prove that no other prior work exists, and it does not change the NO_GO materiality decision.

Additional version check: the [published JHEP PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/JHEP03(2020)006.pdf), printed pages 17–18, explicitly labels the vacuum parametrization Eq. (4.24), the substituted potential Eq. (4.25), and five numerical minimization conditions. This resolves the earlier version-number uncertainty.
