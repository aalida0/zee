# 2005 stationary-depth relation: reproduction and apparent index error

Source: Barroso and Ferreira, PRD 72 (2005) 075010, [hep-ph/0507128v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0507128), Eqs. (2)–(3), (11), (17)–(23). Two complex doublets plus a charged singlet; their singlet has the conjugate charge of the present S+, so identify Φ0=S+*. Their scalar potential is CP conserving and more general than this restricted scalar slice; the relation uses stationary points, independently of Yukawa assumptions. Eq. (11) already covers the pure-singlet stationary energy.

Let X=(s,x,y,u,w), V_M=AᵀX+XᵀBX/2, V=V_M+V_C, and a hat indicate the mixed stationary point. Homogeneity and stationarity yield V_CB=ÂᵀX_CB/2+V_C/4, V_N=AᵀX_N/2; symmetry of B yields X_CBᵀV'_N−X_NᵀV'_CB=2(V_CB−V_N)−V_C/2. The singlet stationarity equation V'_0(CB)=−V_C/(2s) gives the singlet term; the two doublet stationary equations produce the geometric factor. This reproduces the *structure* of Eq. (23).

**Apparent source index typo:** given their own definitions ρ=φ1 and α=φ3, direct expansion gives G=(v1' v2−v2' v1)²+(ρ v2−α v1)². The arXiv v3 text of Eqs. (21)–(23) prints the second square as (v1 ρ−v2 α)². The latter does not follow from the preceding invariants and, on the compatible mixed example below, fails numerically. The corrected G agrees with direct evaluation. We have not located a published erratum, so this is a reproducible apparent discrepancy, not a claim of an author-confirmed erratum.

`eq23_check.py` independently solves the neutral and mixed four-field stationary equations at 65-digit precision. In source complex-component normalization, use ρ=0, α=c, v1'=h, v2'=n, γ=S, and M=Mu4. For the P2 1.5× card, direct ΔV=−729453237.564812 GeV⁴; 2ΔV=−1458906475.12962 GeV⁴. The corrected RHS agrees to relative 1.1×10⁻⁶³; the printed factor gives approximately +2.2364×10¹⁰ GeV⁴. The stationary root is high-precision numerical, not an isolated exact root. Exact independent field-depth inequalities are in `CERTIFICATES/negative_independent.json` and need no stationarity. No claim of a new universal vacuum theorem follows.
