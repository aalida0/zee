# Independent proof and scope

`verify_independent.py` converts original stored binary64 coefficients to exact fractions, verifies their sorted compact JSON SHA-256, constructs the 5×5 matrix from the scalar invariant potential, and checks positive leading principal minors with SymPy exact determinants (Sylvester criterion, no cached LDL data). It finds the neutral root numerically at 80 digits as a proposed center, then proves a rational interval fixed-point inclusion and contraction in a radius 10⁻³⁰ GeV box and checks its position within 10⁻⁸ GeV of the requested EW vev. Exact root, pivot and margin data are in CERTIFICATES. D7 and F13 smallest pivots are 0.148668473145 and 0.104770108173. The respective strict doublet margins are 71557.95 and 482334.34 GeV²; singlet margins 90326.29 and 249954.70 GeV². These margins hold over the complete exact root boxes.

`verify_symbolic_identity.py` independently verifies the invariant square-completion and the general complex Gram identity. `full_field_check.py` builds the ten-real-field polynomial from each term in SARAH LagNoHC/LagHC plus hermitian conjugates and compares value, derivatives and Hessian with the frozen tensor evaluator over 72 complex, boundary and sign-flipped controls; it checks the EW broken-gauge zero modes for both positive cards. The algebra applies without a gauge chart, radial cutoff or fixed sign of Mu4; the residual quadratic is positive definite, the square is nonnegative, and both remaining coefficients are strictly positive. Together, these establish the sufficient all-complex-field tree certificate for these exact restricted potentials, conditional on the explicitly matched SARAH scalar terms. It is not a necessary test and its failure is UNKNOWN.

The independent exact negative-field energy checks, their input roots and exact inequalities are in `negative_witnesses.py` and CERTIFICATES/negative_independent.json. The 2005 stationary comparison is separately numerical, not an exact stationary isolation proof. Nine deliberate tests include quartic sign, large Mu4, ε, neutral tadpole, invalid radius, corrupted pivot, input Mu4-sign tampering, location and positive control. Sign reversal itself is a physical rephasing symmetry, so a hash mismatch is the correct data-integrity failure rather than a claimed instability. An unchecked literal source hash cannot issue a positive certificate.
