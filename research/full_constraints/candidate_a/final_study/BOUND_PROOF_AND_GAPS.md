# Bounds and gaps

`negative_witnesses.py` independently finds a rational neutral-root enclosure and evaluates exact rational energy intervals for the two stored real fields. The fixed-field dependence is affine, ΔV=A+2 μ Re(S*d), because the EW neutral energy is independent of μ. For P2 the stronger field yields threshold 1316.133373038139 GeV, ΔV(1316.13)=+14079.50 and ΔV(1316.14)=−27661.81 GeV⁴; the older witness yields 1346.140510102105 GeV and signs +5395.84 at 1346.14, −100383.78 GeV⁴ at 1346.15. At 1.5×, their gaps are −4.1310×10⁸ and −7.2945×10⁸ GeV⁴. A pure-singlet rational field has gap −5.188×10⁹ GeV⁴. Exact intervals and root boxes are in CERTIFICATES/negative_independent.json. These are sufficient counterexamples, not proofs that the fields are stationary minima. Charged local positivity at these values is checked numerically in the full Hessian; the exact stationary root is enclosed.

For D7, ε=1650627/250000 remains feasible for |Mu4| below approximately 818.87786 GeV; F13_1330 ε=1232051/200000 remains feasible below approximately 1610.18806 GeV. The bounds are sufficient, using exact rational lower root-interval margins, with fixed quartics and positive doublet margin; do not interpret them as maximal feasible ε or physical boundaries. They encompass each family's entire declared path. A sign flip of Mu4 preserves the scalar globality condition by rephasing S; a sign flip is a data-binding falsifier, not a physical instability.

The phase-minimized orbit is x,y,s≥0, u²+w²≤xy and Vmin=V2+V4−2|μ|√[s(xy−u²−w²)]. Every Gram matrix with these inequalities is realizable by two complex doublets (Cholesky factorization), including singular strata, and the singlet magnitude/phase realizes the cubic minimum. `orbit_branch_bound.py` attempted rational interval subdivision for the unresolved P2 k=27 with a recomputed coercive exterior radius 20757 GeV: 2000 boxes processed, 488 infeasible, 507 excluded, 11 boxes remain. It therefore returns UNKNOWN; neither numerical absence nor this unfinished branch-and-bound establishes globality. No maximal witness or exact transition is claimed.

`optimize_witness.py` performs 16 deterministic minimizations of the threshold in a feasible real physical chart; its rounded best field gives 1316.133373038137 GeV and is independently checked with rational intervals in CERTIFICATES/optimized_witness.json. It does not establish global optimality. At 1316.13/1316.14 and 1346.14/1346.15, and at the 1.5× Mu4 case, `negative_witnesses.py` verifies the charged 2×2 principal entry and determinant strictly positive over the exact root interval.
