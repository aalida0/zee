# Evidence changes in this closure attempt

The frozen D14 dataset, D12 and D10 cards, D13/D14 production manifests and prior sector outputs were read, not changed. The 64 D14 scalar/flavor pairs and two D7/F13 NO witnesses have been matched across source-hashed manifests, with scalar/Yukawa content hashes recomputed and D14 production binding digests verified. Their raw production receipt files are unavailable here: a documentary join is not a new validation of the rate calculation. Two additional IO witnesses have verified scalar and Yukawa hashes but no independently verified IO production association. The matrix reports these two separately as unjoined. The 68 are selected witnesses, not a representative population.

Four D7/F13 NO/IO retained dipole sums and F†F entries were recomputed from stored complex matrices, and a coupling-only Cauchy–Schwarz inequality was evaluated. This supplies neither a kernel bound nor a missing two-loop amplitude. The historical B table confidence root was independently interpolated; it remains 473.108 GeV under its explicit Gaussian/table assumptions, not a reproduction of 480 GeV or a two-state exclusion. Eight heavy-scalar propagator extrema and the two conditional Cauchy Higgs-width targets were independently checked against saved fixed-card inputs. They do not establish a complete four-fermion width or Higgs fit.

The source-reported positive tree certificates are reused for all 68 selected candidates at their exact pinned potentials. The new gate leaves unverified joins and incomplete physics unknown; no negative vacuum status is inferred from a failed sufficient test. Scalar BFB/unitarity passes from the D14 source are preserved at their tree-level scope, and oblique-only EWPO is partial. Neutrino target closure is not a NuFIT global likelihood. Other missing evidence is deliberately not promoted to PASS. `SECTOR_ATTEMPTS.md` records the bounded source and materiality attempts and their limitations.
