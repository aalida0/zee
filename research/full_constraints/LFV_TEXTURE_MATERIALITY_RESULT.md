The D7 low_joint texture_0.0001 variants instead exceed the measured limit in the retained subset; its texture_0.001 variants remain below it. This probes one direction, not generic tuning or RG stability, and no conclusion about physical rates follows without omitted terms.

The direct neutral-scalar tree μ→3e diagnostic is numerically tiny, not a full prediction.
