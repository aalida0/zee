## Published-order science scope

1. Original scalar theory: reconstruction, tree BFB, tree unitarity,
   source EWPO, and sufficient D10 tree-vacuum certificates — **16 original**.
2. Leading one-loop neutrino mass inverse, complex source Yukawas
   constructed to suppress neutral `e-μ` Yukawas — **64 deliberately
   constructed** points, not an unbiased parameter-space scan.
3. `B→Xsγ`: Misiak et al arXiv:2608.29863 Eq4 LO-NP dipoles, two
   mixed charged states; one-sided/two-sided Gaussian **screens**.
   The 2026 full NNLO model uncertainty is **not** available.
4. LFV: source-selected scalar one-loop + chosen neutral Barr–Zee dipole
   terms, h125 direct LFV partial widths; original complete
   `mu3e`/conversion operator source has **not** been applied to
   the new Yukawa cards. **Physical LFV = UNKNOWN.**
5. Higgs: source-calculated h125 leading photon/partial widths; full native 64-family on-shell H/A tree channel sums including prominent H/A→H1W and tt are now available but *not* complete total widths or observed limits; the
   positive known-channel sums are **not** full physical widths.
   H/A search rates/HiggsTools for the new cards remain incomplete.
6. Collider: actual `gb→tH2` MG5 5FS LO, source 13 TeV on 16
   scalar cards, source 14 TeV on **three**, no interpolation. Scale/PDF
   QCD theory uncertainty and detector acceptance **unknown**.
7. Event counts after multiplying a published Z dilepton BR still
   require top decays, efficiencies and backgrounds. No confidence
   limits or discoverability.

**Do not describe the 16 scalar cards or 64 flavors as globally allowed,
the observed raw yields as a collider reach, or the algebraic LFV zeros
as a new result.** Our test of flavor changes *at fixed scalar card*
found a <0.1% BR effect for the main three benchmark examples.

## Scope decision

**Ready:** source-defined LO pheno benchmark/collider data and
its data-quality/reviewer review.

**Not established:** an unbiased globally allowed Zee parameter-space
survey, complete leading new-texture `mu3e`/conversion,
heavy H/A HiggsTools observed exclusion, and QCD production
theory systematics or detector-level sensitivity.

The rigorous recommendation is **MAJOR REVISION** for a manuscript
whose central claim is globally allowed 95%-CL collider reach.
A conditional, correctly scoped benchmark methods paper is possible,
but must demonstrate genuine physics beyond the 2017 Zee flavor scans,
2021 charged cascade and 2023 Yukawa texture constructions.
