# Zγ source-to-native chirality review

The source-gate test was rerun on 8 November 2026 against the SHA-256-pinned author implementation. Four independent f3/f4 quadrature pairs passed, with a worst relative difference of 1.04e-14. Two one-sided native routing assertions passed. These are six controls, not a validation of either full complex source amplitude. The test does not independently execute the source's full cL/cR functions, and its `source_eq54_R_for_D_e_mu` field is an algebraic statement under the supplied map, not a numerical source-code result.

The conflict is directly visible at the Lagrangian level. In arXiv:2410.17313v4 Eq. (11), `(rho_l dagger)_{e mu}` multiplies `bar e P_R mu`, and Eq. (54) places `rho_l,21*` in c_R. The supplied mapping identifies that entry with `D[e,mu]/sqrt(2)`. The frozen native definition in `module3/core.py::vertices` instead puts the same `D[e,mu]` entry in the coefficient of `bar e P_L mu`, and `module4/amplitudes.py::ledger` follows that routing. Both dipole actions specify the projector acting on the incoming muon. This is an internal inconsistency among the asserted mapping, native vertices, and ordered operators; the test does not determine which convention should be revised. Matching may require a conjugate transpose and a consistently rederived CP-odd and quark coupling map, but applying that conjecture without an independent signed photon-subset comparison would not validate interference.

No new Zγ card amplitudes or branching ratios are calculated. The prior source-gate report and existing baseline hashes remain the operative artifacts. The 2026 erratum is identified by DOI 10.1007/JHEP07(2026)184; its equations were not independently inspected in this review. The v4 paper explicitly notes that Eqs. (54)–(55) were corrected, and the tests compare sampled loop functions to the pinned code, not the full erratum against the implementation. LFV remains UNKNOWN. A decisive next check is an independent source-to-native match of the ordered complex photon-fermion amplitude, including both chiralities, CP-even/odd phases and relative sign, followed by complete Zγ controls against the corrected source implementation.
