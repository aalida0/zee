# Integrated LFV: incomplete execution

The four frozen cards have a coherent baseline + previously validated neutral-fermion Zγ ledger. The independently assembled CP-conserving hZ expression was compared to the pinned author-code hZ assignment for two nonzero complex controls, both chiralities: relative differences are 3.13e-6 and 3.37e-6. The archived f7 differences used mZ=91.1876 GeV, while the author example uses 91.188 GeV; this is a plausible source of the difference, but it has not been separately demonstrated. The 1e-4 tolerance is a diagnostic tolerance, not a high-precision source validation. Required one-sided, alignment and independent loop-function checks remain unexecuted. Thus the hZ contribution is null in the validated ledger; its provisional source output and coherent partial-rate diagnostic are retained explicitly. No new group is certified.

The source is Altmannshofer et al., [arXiv:2410.17313v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.17313v4), Eq. (59), [JHEP 06 (2025) 156](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2025)156), with [erratum JHEP 07 (2026) 184](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP07(2026)184). The fixed source hash and card hashes are in INPUT_MANIFEST.json. The hZ controls use the existing preliminary quadrature, not a newly independent integral. The source-code hZ assignment is extracted by AST; the alternate assembly contracts the CP-conserving neutral mixing and archived quadrature. The repeated NO/IO controls use identical spectra, so they are two distinct spectra, not four independent controls. The author source EW inputs and original baseline use different input schemes. No uncertainty for this difference is assigned.

The previously validated Zγ data were read without rerunning their calculation. Photon terms are already in the baseline and are not added again. DIPOLE_TERMS.json records provisional hZ and rate changes; those rates are illustrative partial sums, not full branching fractions or exclusions. The scalar/Yukawa identifiers are taken from the prior pinned replay. MATERIALITY.json preserves the coupling invariants and conditional triangle-inequality missing-norm budgets; those are not bounds on missing amplitudes. All global acceptances remain null.

The complete source-matched three-lepton and semileptonic operator vectors, signed phases, running, and nuclear interference were not computed in this execution. Prior F-only box and contact checks do not establish them. The documented upstream scalar proton/neutron interchange was not corrected or tested here. Therefore mu→3e and Al conversion remain UNKNOWN. Tau modes, universality and h→μe are not assessed. Missing electroweak groups and F-gauge contributions cannot be assumed zero or bounded by the size of hZ.

This execution does not establish a paper-ready complete leading-LFV prescription. A paper could describe the retained subset and state conditional bounds, provided it makes no unqualified allowed/excluded claim. See TRIAGE.md. No new parameter points or scans were produced.
