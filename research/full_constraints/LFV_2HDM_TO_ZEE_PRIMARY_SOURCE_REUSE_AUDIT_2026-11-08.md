# LFV amplitude-level literature and code-reuse audit: 2HDM → Zee Type-II (8 Nov 2026)

**Scope and evidence:** Independent literature/source check against the current \`aalida0/zee\` branch \`research/zee-full-constraints\`, starting from commit \`7901fa06ad3ed7feb734fa9ba3a13338ddf6d4be\`, using hep-research scientific-validation rules, arXiv/published full-text passages, alphaXiv PDF queries, Undermind's preexisting 75-paper Zee two-hop corpus and source reports, and the existing code/receipt inventory. **No new amplitude was calculated in this review**; no frozen physics modules or existing receipts were altered. No proof of exhaustiveness or absence of a complete Zee calculation is claimed. This document is a **reuse decision** for the restricted CP-conserving 2HDM with Type-II quark sector, arbitrary complex lepton Yukawa, antisymmetric singlet \`F\`, and two mixed physical charged scalars; it does not authorize a full scan or change existing PHYSICAL_UNKNOWN statuses.

## Decision: substantially reuse 2HDM, but choose an explicit perturbative accuracy target

**GO for a scoped leading-contribution LFV implementation; NOT YET GO for a complete physical LFV exclusion or a claimed fully constrained scan.** The most promising approach is (i) preserve already validated one-loop \`D/F\` and neutral amplitudes; (ii) use the **post-erratum** general-2HDM leading two-loop results for source-matched doublet/neutral sectors; (iii) add Zee-specific *leading* structures with \`F\`, mixing, unequal charged masses and off-diagonal \`Z H_1 H_2\` currents; (iv) assess quantitatively whether omitted gauge-dressed \`F†F\` two-loop corrections and higher loops can be consistently treated as beyond the targeted accuracy. **Such suppression is a testable power-counting/phenomenology hypothesis, not an independently proven error bound**. A complete two-loop Zee model prediction is a different, more costly claim and is **not needed by default** for a sensible scoped phenomenology calculation.

Crucial methodological precedent: Altmannshofer et al. expressly retain leading two-loop terms quadratic in the Yukawas but **omit two-loop corrections to Yukawa combinations already occurring at one loop** (arXiv:2410.17313v4, preceding Eq. (53); discussion before Eq. (20)). They do **not** claim every two-loop correction to the one-loop coefficients. Transfer the **declared accuracy**, not a false claim of strict all-two-loop completeness.

## Exact conventions to freeze first

From native \`LFV_COVERAGE.md\`:
\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{dip}}= \frac{e m_{\mu}}{2}\bar e\,\sigma^{\mu\nu}(C_L P_L+C_R P_R)\,\mu\,F_{\mu\nu}+\mathrm{h.c.},
\quad [C]=\mathrm{GeV}^{-2}.
\]
Altmannshofer et al. arXiv:2410.17313v4 Eq. (48) uses \(-e m_\mu c_X/(16\pi^2 v^2)\), hence **\(C_X=-c_X/(8\pi^2 v^2)\)** with compatible external flavor ordering. Do not conflate \`C_L/C_R\` with the ordered FlavorKit \`K2\` bridge or swap chirality by eye.

The native frozen \`F=2 f_{\rm Chen}\`, \`D=-\sin\beta\,Y_e+\cos\beta\,Y_{e2}\`. For the **physical 2021 Zee rotation convention**:
\[
H_D^+=\cos\gamma\,H_1^+-\sin\gamma\,H_2^+,\qquad
S^+=\sin\gamma\,H_1^++\cos\gamma\,H_2^+ .
\]
Thus doublet and singlet projection vectors are \(d=(\cos\gamma,-\sin\gamma)\) and \(s=(\sin\gamma,\cos\gamma)\), respectively. Each vertex must be rotated with its own complex flavor/mass/chirality indices. Two mass eigenstates must be summed at **amplitude** level before squaring. Purely doubly doublet-Yukawa terms with diagonal charged propagation may acquire \(d_i^2\) weights, but that is **not** a universal rule for an entire 2HDM diagram family; gauge/potential and mixed-\(F\) vertices must be examined separately.

Florentino–Romão–Silva (2021), App. B.1 and App. D.3 Eq. (D.20), explicitly exhibit nonzero off-diagonal \(Z H_1^+H_2^-\) and unequal-mass scalar kernels; photon charge is diagonal. This makes the 2HDM \`HZ\`, \`HW\` and \(f_{8e}\) **nontrivial Zee gauge-group extensions**. This fact was already known to our 2026 source rescue; do not treat it as a new discovery.

## Important paper restrictions (verified from full text)

1. **Herrero-García et al., JHEP04(2017)130, arXiv:1701.05345v2**: despite “full parameter scan” in title, *charged LFV contributions are leading/approximate*, with a selected lepton texture including \(f^{e\mu}=0\) and restricted direct \(Y_{2,e\mu},Y_{2,\mu e}\); the quark assumption is **\(Y_{u2}=Y_{d2}=0\)** rather than user's Type-II quark assignment. Radiative two-loop is attributed to 1993 neutral Barr–Zee. Its \(\mu\to e\) conversion follows a photonic monopole approximation; it does not provide every 4-lepton or semileptonic operator for present Type-II cards. Reproduce matching limits, not transfer the model's scan exclusions wholesale. Use **published section-style equation references**, not the arXiv HTML's running numerical equation labels (e.g. published \((3.13)–(3.19)\)).
2. **Heeck–Thapa, PLB841(2023)137910, arXiv:2303.13383v2**: general complex \(Y\), antisymmetric \(f\) in Higgs basis, but neglects extra-doublet quark Yukawas and assumes alignment. States that two-loop \(Z\), charged-scalar-loop and \(WW H_1/H_1 H_2H_2\) diagrams are neglected in its considered setup; trileptons use neutral-scalar **tree** graphs, conversion uses its **dipole-only** Eq. (5). Those restrictions need not hold for Type-II quarks or sizeable charged mixing.
3. **Altmannshofer et al., JHEP06(2025)156 + JHEP07(2026)184 erratum**, arXiv:2410.17313v4 (May 2026): the *author's complete leading two-loop 2HDM* is not a complete Zee two-loop result. The erratum changes neutral fermion dipole equations (published \((5.7),(5.8)\), arXiv Eqs. (54)–(55)); use the post-erratum pinned source, not original print equations. Existing \`thdm.py\` pinned commit \`b5035cd58d2a7e4a91e9790d045df062b97adffe\`, SHA-256 \`9b4af7aa4bf7bf533a7b82c8c2603d118346424e3b7b5f9f06ab8cb41bc4e193\`; post-erratum version and specific additional HW anomaly in \`LFV_COVERAGE.md\`. The present audit independently verified that the published **erratum exists**, but did **not** reproduce its exact full formula diffs or the HW one-particle correction.
4. **Mitsuda–Sasaki, PLB516(2001)47, hep-ph/0103202**: mixed charged scalar \(F\)-only box includes diagonal and off-diagonal scalar-mass combinations via logarithmic \(J(M_i^2,M_j^2)\). Old simplifying assumption of **incoherent box vs photon rate** (paper Eq. 28) is not valid for claiming full complex Zee interference.
5. **Ghosal–Koide–Fusaoka, PRD64(2001)053012, hep-ph/0104104**: its one-loop \(Z\ell_i\ell_j\) form factor is given **on shell**; our desired low-q off-shell \(\mu\to3e\)/conversion form factor must be matched separately.
6. **Bilenky–Santamaria, NPB420(1994)47, hep-ph/9310302**: valuable gauge-invariant *heavy unmixed charged-singlet* EFT source; cannot be pasted into the two-state mixed model without EFT-scale/hierarchy assumptions.
7. **Porod–Staub–Vicente, EPJC74(2014)2992, arXiv:1405.1434** and **Khasianevich et al., CPC302(2024)109244**: generic one-loop coefficient *generators*, not automatically validated complete Zee predictions. Our generated FlavorKit conversion's scalar nucleon contraction is **exchanged**, confirmed against KKO and independent FlexibleSUSY contraction. FlexibleSUSY's prior build attempt stopped at FeynArts/FormCalc version prerequisites before generating any Zee graph.

## Cheapest decisive follow-up, rather than another broad calculation

**First priority: one bounded *source-matched leading-LFV perturbative-scope test* on the 4 D7/F13 NO/IO saved witnesses, no new cards.** Implement only missing leading 2HDM **neutral Zγ + \(hZ\)** groups and source-compatible **charged fermion-\(W\)** family *if directly transferable*; maintain a term-by-term \((C_L,C_R)\) complex ledger. Test \`F=0,\gamma=0\`, charged state exchange, pure singlet, rephasing, \(\mu\) versus \(\tau\) masses and two independent paper/author-code normalizations; protect the already-validated photon subset against double counting.

**Second priority: classify the genuinely new Zee \(F\)-gauge contributions by *leading vs radiative correction* at the chosen accuracy.** Draw the external gauge/mixing/Goldstone topology list and identify flavor invariants: \(F^\dagger F\) (already nonzero at one loop), \(F D^\dagger\) or \(D F^\dagger\) (zero at one loop in the frozen massless-\(\nu\) dipole limit but possibly different at higher orders), and mixed charged-scalar/potential insertions. Determine whether unsuppressed/chirality-enhanced new invariants enter at leading two loops. A **qualitative loop-factor guess is not a numerical bound**; if the chosen publication claim insists on all leading diagrams, any such uncalculated leading source remains UNKNOWN. If absent at the explicitly declared leading accuracy and all retained pieces have source-validated bounds, the paper can state that accuracy **without ever claiming full two-loop Zee**. Avoid a complete gauge renormalization calculation unless this very check makes it demonstrably necessary.

**Third priority (separate problem):** independent complete one-loop coefficient generator for \(\mu\to3e\)/conversion using corrected frozen SARAH vertices, 2024 FlexibleSUSY after isolated **FeynArts≥3.10, FormCalc≥9.8** setup, and an independent rate backend (flavio or MuonBridge); do not inherit the existing conversion nucleon sign swap.

**Stop condition:** produce a machine-readable 4-card table: each complex \`C_L/C_R\` term, source equation, inclusion, physically declared perturbative order, interference and justified remainder. Give one of \`LEADING_LFV_SUPPORTED_IN_DECLARED_SCOPE\`, \`LEADING_LFV_UNRESOLVED\`, \`SOURCE_OR_CODE_DISCREPANCY\`; preserve global physical UNKNOWN unless all mandatory CLFV modes are resolved. **No more multi-thousand-point scan, 24-Codex-round loop, or speculative full two-loop derivation by default.**
