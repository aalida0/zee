# Operator conventions and boundaries

Indices denote μ→e, with native lepton order e, μ, τ. Complex numbers are `[real,imag]` unless explicitly stored as `{real,imag}`. All listed coefficients have dimension GeV⁻². The radiative native action is `L = e m_mu/2 bar(e) sigma_munu (C_L P_L + C_R P_R) mu F^munu + h.c.` (Module3 `OPERATOR`); its baseline includes Module3 and Module4, and the validated neutral Zγ group is separately recorded. Do not add it twice. These quantities are at the frozen source matching convention, not evolved low-energy Wilsons.

For neutral scalar exchange, `module3.core.vertices` defines `L = -S bar(e)_a (C_ab P_L + C_ba* P_R)e_b`. The direct amplitude coefficient `S_XY = sum_S C_eμ^X C_ee^Y/m_S²`, where `C^L_ab=C_ab`, `C^R_ab=C_ba*`, is recorded with the ordered direct bilinears `(bar e P_X mu)(bar e P_Y e)`. The identical electron exchange is subtracted in the amplitude, with the symmetry factor in phase space. Existing frozen tree rate is a diagnostic. The tree and dipole entries are source-convention subsets, not a complete vector.

The restricted charged-singlet box raw generated `BO4lVLL` and opposite-sign paper Lagrangian coefficient are both given explicitly. Mitsuda–Sasaki [hep-ph/0103202](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0103202), Eqs. 16, 26–27, applies in the restricted F-only limit. The raw generated ordered-action phase relative to other source penguins is **not** established. The total coefficient of VLL is null.

The intended general off-shell photon basis is `e bar(e)[q² gamma^μ(K1L PL+K1R PR)+i m_mu sigma^μν q_ν(K2L PL+K2R PR)]mu A_mu`, and contact structures are `(bar e Gamma_X mu)(bar e Gamma_Y e)` for S,V,T, plus corresponding quark scalar/vector and gluonic operators. The overall action sign and the conversion between K2 and native C are **not matched** in this adapter; K1/K2 common-basis values, Z, full S/V/T and quark/gluon coefficients are null. A definition alone does not validate matching, phases, RG scale or evolution.

`al_kernel` uses the *distinct* pinned FlexibleSUSY source convention: amplitudes `L=DR D/4-gpLV Vp-gnLV Vn-gpRS Sp-gnRS Sn`, `R=DL D/4-gpRV Vp-gnRV Vn-gpLS Sp-gnLS Sn`; rate `4(|L|²+|R|²)/Gamma_capture`. Source-specific coefficients are GeV⁻², overlaps are dimension GeV^(5/2), and Γ is GeV. This code refuses null inputs. It is a numerical response kernel, not a native-to-FlexibleSUSY Wilson map. It is based on the pinned source and its cited [conversion formalism](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06650), with separate KKO-like p/n overlaps [hep-ph/0203110](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0203110). No physical rate is implied by synthetic zero insertions.
