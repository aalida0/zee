# Remaining matching

1. μ→3e: calculate and independently match complex off-shell K1L/R, Z penguins and all relevant D/F charged and neutral boxes in one common ordered action with K2 and tree scalars; verify interference and identical-electron rate. Restricted F-only VLL is source controlled but its cross-sector phase is unresolved.
2. Conversion: match photon, Z, Type-II h/H/A scalar and semileptonic boxes to the kernel convention; include heavy-quark/gluon thresholds and QCD/QED running. Fixing the p/n response alone does not supply the UV coefficients.
3. Radiative: retained Module3/4 plus neutral Zγ are a signed subset. New potentially enhanced mixed F–D charged-current structures remain unresolved; no full general-Rξ calculation was attempted. Standard F†F dressing is beyond the declared one-loop order without being a bound on different structures.

All three physical rates and LFV acceptance remain unknown. A qualified cascade study is possible without claiming these processes as completed constraints.
