## Required independent physics gates
A. **Physical->Lagrangian reconstruction:** use Module 0 checked conventions. Validate tadpoles, Goldstones, CP-even/odd/charged Hessians, eigenmasses and mixing, nontrivial signs/angles; use independently differentiated polynomial not circular reuse. Multiple points including edge cases.
B. **BFB:** include *all* scalar quartics including K12 and charged-singlet directions, neutral+charged complex-field orientations. Implement mathematically justified **sufficient** positivity certification and numerical negative-direction witnesses, independently verify with high-precision/alternative parametrization. Treat inconclusive as UNKNOWN, not allowed or excluded. Do not silently call a merely sufficient BFB condition necessary.
C. **Tree perturbative unitarity:** evaluate complete canonically normalized two-particle scalar 55x55 tree contact-scattering matrix for 10 real degrees, validate combinatorial factors, block charges/multiplicities, basis invariance, eigenvalues and normalizations, check largest partial-wave eigenvalue against explicitly documented 8*pi convention. Independently cross-check decoupling limits / existing Gate6 sector screens; preserve any genuine disagreement.
D. **Vacuum:** minimize *full* tree potential (including dimensionful cubic Mu4, soft mass m12^2, singlet and charge-breaking directions), audit gauge redundancies, stationary gradients and Hessian, use reproducible multistart/alternative seeds and independent potential evaluation. A credible deeper stationary minimum is an exclusion **witness**; absence of one is NOT a proof of global stability. Return VACUUM_NO_DEEPER_WITNESS_FOUND or VACUUM_DEEPER_WITNESS, never globally stable merely from numerical sampling.
E. **Scalar one-loop EWPO:** reuse Grimus-based Gate A1g/A1h S,T,U predictions and published covariance fits, cross-check charged-pair **-2** coefficient vs the -1/2 literature discrepancy, mixing conventions, SM subtraction, decoupling, zero splitting, quadrature convergence, covariance treatment and versions of experimental inputs. Clearly label scalar oblique-only likelihood, not full electroweak acceptance. Do not combine alternative correlated fits as independent measurements.

Strictly distinguish **restricted CP-conserving Zee scalar potential with complex lepton Yukawas** from the most general renormalizable Zee scalar potential: lambda6/7 absent, not proven RGE-closed; do not claim generic RG stability. Optional RG diagnostic must be labeled supplementary until genuinely supported by full consistent beta functions.
